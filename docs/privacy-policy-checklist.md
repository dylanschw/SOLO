# SOLO Privacy Policy Checklist

This is a drafting checklist, not legal advice. Review it before App Store, Google Play, ads, donations, payments, or native health sync.

## Data SOLO Stores Today

- Account identifier and profile preferences.
- Workout programs, workout sessions, exercises, and logged sets.
- Nutrition logs, targets, user-created recipes, and user-created meal prep templates.
- Bodyweight entries.
- Scheduling tasks, routine items, water logs, and creatine completion.
- Manual health metrics such as sleep, steps, resting heart rate, calories burned, water, and creatine.

## Required Policy Topics

- What data is collected and why.
- Whether data is sold or shared.
- Supabase hosting/authentication as a service provider.
- User control over editing, deleting, and exporting data.
- Account deletion and data deletion support path.
- Data retention for soft-deleted records and backups.
- Security practices and limits.
- Contact email for privacy requests.

## Future Health Data Requirements

Actual Apple Health, Apple Watch, Google Fit, or Health Connect sync needs explicit privacy language before release.

- List every health data type read or written.
- Explain that health data is used for SOLO dashboard and goal summaries.
- Request only necessary permissions.
- Describe permission denial and revocation behavior.
- Complete App Store privacy labels and Google Play Data Safety answers for health and fitness data.

## Future Monetization Requirements

SOLO currently has no ads, payments, or donations. Before adding them:

- Document ad SDKs or payment processors.
- Add consent flows where required.
- Explain optional paid removal or donation handling.
- Keep ads non-blocking for workouts and logging.
